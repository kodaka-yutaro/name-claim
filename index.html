Can you name something nobody has?

How it works

Type any name and press the button. If nobody on this site has ever proposed exactly that name, you win and it is recorded as yours. If someone already did, you see how many times it has been proposed.

Names are compared exactly as typed: “Alice” and “alice” are different names. Each device counts once per name.

Every name you claim first moves into your town as a new resident. The more residents you have, the bigger your town grows, from a lonely campfire to the capital of the world.
